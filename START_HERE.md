---

## ✅ Quick Fix (3 Minutes)

### The Problem:
Firebase Authentication is not enabled in your Firebase Console.

### The Solution:
Follow these 3 simple steps:

---

### Step 1️⃣: Go to Firebase Console

1. Open: https://console.firebase.google.com
2. Click on your project: **farmerdb-ba9b0**

---

### Step 2️⃣: Enable Email/Password Authentication

1. Click **"Authentication"** in left sidebar
2. Click **"Sign-in method"** tab
3. Find **"Email/Password"** in the list
4. Click on it
5. Toggle **"Enable"** ON
6. Click **"Save"**

✅ **Authentication is now enabled!**

---

### Step 3️⃣: Create Your First User

1. Click **"Users"** tab (still in Authentication)
2. Click **"Add user"** button
3. Enter:
   - Email: `admin@dcs.com`
   - Password: `admin123`
4. Click **"Add user"**

✅ **User created!**

---

## 🎉 Now Try Login Again!

Go back to DCS Pro and login with:
- **Email**: admin@dcs.com
- **Password**: admin123

**You should now be logged in!** 🎊
