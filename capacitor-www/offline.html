Internet chahiye

Internet connection needed

DCS Pro pehli baar kholne ke liye internet zaroori hai.

DCS Pro needs internet the first time it opens.

Ek baar khul jaane ke baad app bina internet ke bhi chalegi — entries phone mein save hongi aur internet aane par apne aap sync hongi.

After that the app works offline — entries are saved on the phone and sync automatically when internet returns.
